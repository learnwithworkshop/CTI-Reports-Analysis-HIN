# Recorded Future

**Recorded Future** is a leading **AI-powered Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** company that helps organizations proactively detect, analyze, prioritize, and respond to cyber threats before they become security incidents.

The company combines Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and large-scale data collection to continuously monitor billions of data points across the Open Web, Deep Web, Dark Web, technical sources, malware repositories, vulnerability databases, and threat actor communities.

Today, Recorded Future is considered one of the world's largest and most trusted Threat Intelligence providers, serving governments, Fortune 500 companies, financial institutions, healthcare organizations, and critical infrastructure operators worldwide.

---

# Overview

Recorded Future's mission is to help security teams stay ahead of cyber adversaries by transforming massive amounts of raw security data into actionable intelligence.

Unlike traditional threat feeds that simply provide Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), Recorded Future enriches intelligence with context, relationships, confidence scores, timelines, and risk prioritization.

Its AI-powered platform enables Security Operations Centers (SOC), Threat Intelligence (CTI), Incident Response (IR), Vulnerability Management, Fraud Detection, and Executive Security teams to make faster and more informed decisions.

Recorded Future's flagship platform is the **Recorded Future Intelligence Cloud**.

The platform continuously collects, analyzes, correlates, and enriches threat data from thousands of intelligence sources using AI and machine learning.
