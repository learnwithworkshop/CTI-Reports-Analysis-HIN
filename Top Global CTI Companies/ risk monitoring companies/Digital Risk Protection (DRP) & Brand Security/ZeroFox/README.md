# ZeroFox

**ZeroFox** ek leading **External Cybersecurity** aur **Digital Risk Protection (DRP)** company hai jo organizations ko internet ke bahar maujood cyber threats se protect karne mein help karti hai.

Traditional cybersecurity solutions mainly internal network ko protect karte hain, lekin ZeroFox organization ke **external attack surface** ko monitor karta hai. Isme social media platforms, domains, websites, phishing campaigns, dark web, deep web, mobile apps, impersonation accounts aur leaked credentials jaise external sources continuously monitor kiye jaate hain.

ZeroFox Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Natural Language Processing (NLP) aur human intelligence ka combination use karke cyber threats ko detect, analyze aur mitigate karta hai.

Aaj ZeroFox ko duniya ki leading **Digital Risk Protection (DRP)** aur **External Attack Surface Management (EASM)** companies mein se ek mana jata hai.

---

# Overview

Aaj ke time mein organizations ka sirf internal network hi target nahi hota.

Attackers fake social media accounts bana kar brand impersonation karte hain, phishing websites create karte hain, employees ke credentials leak karte hain aur dark web par sensitive information sell karte hain.

ZeroFox ka mission hai organizations ko internet ke har external corner se continuously protect karna.

Platform real-time monitoring, automated detection aur analyst-driven investigation ke through organizations ko actionable intelligence provide karta hai.
