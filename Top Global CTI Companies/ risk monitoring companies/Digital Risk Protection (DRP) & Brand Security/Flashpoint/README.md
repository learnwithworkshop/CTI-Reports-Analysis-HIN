# Flashpoint

**Flashpoint** ek leading **Risk Intelligence Company** hai jo organizations ko cyber, physical, fraud aur national security threats ko proactively detect, samajhne aur unka response dene mein help karti hai.

---

# Overview

Flashpoint human-powered data collection, analyst-driven intelligence aur intuitive technology ko combine karta hai taaki mission-critical organizations apne logon (people), locations (places) aur digital/physical assets ko protect kar sakein.

Iska platform sirf raw data provide nahi karta, balki us data ko analyze karke **finished intelligence**, real-time alerts aur analytics bhi ek hi unified platform ke andar deliver karta hai.

---

# Company Profile

Flashpoint khud ko ek globally trusted **Risk Intelligence Leader** ke roop mein position karta hai.

Ye duniya bhar ki private companies aur government organizations ko fast aur comprehensive visibility provide karta hai, taaki wo rapidly badhte cyber aur security threats ko samay par identify aur handle kar sakein.

---

# Core Technology & Platform

Flashpoint ka flagship platform **Flashpoint Ignite** hai.

Ye ek award-winning Threat Intelligence Platform hai jo primary source collection aur analyst enrichment par based hai.

Flashpoint Ignite cyber threats, vulnerabilities, physical security aur national security intelligence ko ek hi workspace mein combine karta hai, jisse security teams ko alag-alag tools use karne ki zarurat nahi padti.

---

## Key Capabilities

Flashpoint Ignite ki kuch important capabilities:

* Human-powered primary source collection.
* Expert analysts ke dwara intelligence ko enrich aur validate karna.
* Finished intelligence, alerting aur analytics provide karna.
* Different security teams ke beech collaboration enable karna.
* APIs ke through data integration aur automation support dena.

Shuruat mein Flashpoint mainly ek **Cyber Threat Intelligence Company** tha.
