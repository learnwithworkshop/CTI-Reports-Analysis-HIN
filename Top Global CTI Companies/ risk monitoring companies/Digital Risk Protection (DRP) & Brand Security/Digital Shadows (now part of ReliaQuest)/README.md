# ReliaQuest (formerly Digital Shadows) - Corporate Overview

## 📌 Introduction
ReliaQuest is a global leader in cybersecurity that helps organizations manage risk, detect threats, and respond to incidents. In 2022, ReliaQuest acquired Digital Shadows for $160 million, integrating its world-class Digital Risk Protection (DRP) and Threat Intelligence capabilities into its core operations.

---

## 🏢 Company Profile


| Attribute | Details |

| **Industry** | Cybersecurity, Threat Intelligence, Digital Risk Protection (DRP) |
| **Founded** | ReliaQuest: 2007 | Digital Shadows: 2011 |
| **Founders** | ReliaQuest: Brian Murphy | Digital Shadows: Alastair Paterson & James Chappell |
| **Headquarters** | Tampa, Florida, USA (Global HQ) |
| **Regional Offices** | London (UK), Dublin (Ireland), Salt Lake City (USA), Pune (India) |
| **Current Status** | Active (Digital Shadows operates under the ReliaQuest brand) |

---

## 🛠️ Core Technology & Platform: GreyMatter

Digital Shadows' technology has been fully integrated into ReliaQuest's flagship cloud-native security operations platform, known as **GreyMatter**.

### Key Capabilities:
* **Digital Risk Protection (DRP):** Monitors the open, deep, and dark web to identify exposed credentials, data leakage, and brand impersonation.
* **Threat Intelligence:** Provides actionable insights on global cyber threats, threat actors, and vulnerabilities.
* **Detection & Response (XDR):** Automates threat detection and speeds up response times across multi-cloud and on-premises environments.
* **Security Operations Center (SOC) Visibility:** Gives security teams a unified view of their entire security ecosystem without moving data.

---

## 🕒 Timeline & Evolution

* **2007:** ReliaQuest is founded by Brian Murphy to solve enterprise security visibility challenges.
* **2011:** Digital Shadows is founded in London, focusing on cyber situational awareness and dark web monitoring.
* **June 2022:** ReliaQuest officially acquires Digital Shadows for $160 million to combine threat intelligence with automated response.
* **Post-2022:** Digital Shadows' SearchLight platform is rebranded and merged into ReliaQuest GreyMatter.

## 💼 Business Model & Target Market

* **Target Customers:** Fortune 1000 companies, large enterprises, healthcare sectors, financial institutions, and government agencies.
* **Service Delivery:** Delivered as a SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) model combined with managed security services (SOC-as-a-Service).
